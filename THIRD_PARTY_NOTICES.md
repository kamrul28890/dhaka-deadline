## Grab3D three-wheeler

Tuk-Tuk / Cozy Cars Kit: https://grab3d.com/models/vehicles/tuk-tuk/

The publisher releases this model and its textures under CC0 1.0, without a purchase or account requirement. Download provenance: https://grab3d.com/dl/tuk-tuk/manifest.json. The source GLB is pinned by SHA256; source and converted hashes are recorded in `assets/manifest.json`. Wheel/handlebar names are normalized for animation. The game adapts scale and colors and adds rear brake lenses. This is a generic three-wheeler, not a verified replica of a Bangladeshi CNG model.

Bus proportions are fitted to the existing collision footprint, boarding doors are animated, shop signs use fictional Bangla names and generic pedestrian models replace the capsule visuals. These adaptations do not establish geographic or demographic accuracy.
